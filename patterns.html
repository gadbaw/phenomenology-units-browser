1 · The century of the analyst's voice

Whose in-session experience does psychoanalytic writing render, by decade? The dyadic register climbs from ~5% (1910s) to ~27% today — the relational turn, visible as a curve.

clinician patient dyadic — share of in-session moments. Classical authors dated to their original era; others by filename year. Early decades are thin.

2 · The anatomy of change

Where each role falls across a healing arc (left=start, right=end). Strain & enactment front-load; turn and meeting cluster at the end — and turn→meeting is the single most common transition in mutative arcs.
